The dashed arrows are the exceptions, not the default path: a small CSV or a raw Avro stream can be read directly by an engine, but for repeated analytical queries at scale, raw row-based data is first converted to Parquet (the ETL step) rather than queried in place. Arrow isn't a separate destination engines send data to — decoding into Arrow buffers is literally what "reading the file" means inside the engine, shown here as a step inside the engine box. Feather is a side-door: an optional snapshot of those in-memory Arrow buffers written to disk for fast handoff to another Arrow-aware tool, not part of the main storage path.
